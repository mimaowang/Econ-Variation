# CER (China Economic Review) batch triage

Source: Crossref ISSN 1043-951X, most recently indexed 60 articles.

## Triage rules
- Keep only sources with a clearly identifiable policy, institutional assignment, or experimental variation that affects Chinese units.
- Skip purely descriptive, correlational, or endogenous firm/individual behavior studies.
- Avoid duplicating existing canonical records.
- Mark promising candidates for screen tasks.

## Skipped
- Weather/climate/health descriptive studies (#1, #4, #5, #8, #22, #50) — no institutional assignment.
- RCTs/field experiments (#6, #11, #23) — method valuable but too micro or program-specific; evaluate later if method transfer needed.
- Culture/history/preference studies (#27, #44, #48) — not current policy variation.
- Trade/GVC/comparative studies (#12, #14, #24, #35, #42, #56) — mostly foreign shocks or simulation; China exposure often diffuse.
- Labor/education descriptive or robot adoption studies (#13, #17, #21, #25, #28, #29) — endogenous technology/adoption or vague variation.
- Corporate finance without clear policy shock (#3 green M&A, #10 female leadership, #16, #20, #38, #45, #46, #52, #54, #55).
- Editorial boards, corrigenda, and non-empirical pieces.
